If you found something

Stay calm. A spot worth a closer look is not proof, but it is worth taking seriously.

  • 1. Leave it where it is

    Do not touch or move the device. Take photos from where you are.

  • 2. Keep yourself safe

    If you feel unsafe, leave the room.

  • 3. Tell someone who can act

    • The warden, hotel or PG manager
    • Your college's Internal Complaints Committee

  • 4. Emergency numbers

    Emergency: 112. Women's helpline: 181.

    Call 112 Call 181
