If you found something

Stay calm. A spot worth a closer look is not proof, but it is worth taking seriously.

What to do
  • 1

    Leave it where it is

    Do not touch or move the device. Take photos from where you are.

  • 2

    Keep yourself safe

    If you feel unsafe, leave the room.

  • 3

    Save the report

    The PDF lists what was checked and when, with your saved photos.

  • 4

    Tell someone who can act

    The warden, hotel or PG manager, or your college's Internal Complaints Committee.

Emergency numbers
  • Emergency112
  • Women's helpline181

Both numbers are free to call from any phone in India.
