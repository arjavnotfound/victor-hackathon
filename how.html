How it works

Every check sends something into the room and watches what answers back.

Lens check

  • A lens sends light back

    A camera lens focuses light onto a flat sensor. Some of that light bounces straight back toward where it came from, like a cat's eyes in headlights. Your torch sits next to your camera, so a lens shows up as a small bright dot.

  • A secret pattern

    The torch blinks a new random pattern every scan. A dot that blinks in exactly that pattern is reflecting your torch. A lamp, a screen or a standby light cannot copy a pattern it has never seen.

  • Round and small

    A lens shows up as a small round dot. Shine off a spoon, a tap or a frame edge is usually a streak or a big patch, so it is ignored. Turn on "Show ignored reflections" in Details to see what was ignored and why.

  • A second look

    A dot found again after you move a little is marked "seen twice, worth a closer look". Hold the phone between arm's length and about 1 metre from the area.

  • Which way you pointed

    The small ring on the camera screen fills in as you point the phone in different directions, using its compass. It shows where you looked, not that a direction is clear.

Night-vision lights

  • Light you cannot see

    Many cameras have infrared lights for recording in the dark. Your eyes cannot see them, but a phone camera often shows them as white or purple dots. In a dark room, the app looks for small dots that stay on or blink steadily.

Network (Android app)

  • Names and devices

    The app lists nearby Wi-Fi networks and devices on your Wi-Fi. It looks for default camera hotspot names, devices that use video ports, and devices that answer or announce themselves with camera messages. Each one comes with the reasons, in plain words.

  • Does it stream video?

    For a device with a video port open, the app asks it the first question a video player would ask. A device that answers like a video stream is a strong sign.

  • Find it

    For a camera that runs its own Wi-Fi hotspot, "Find it" follows that hotspot's signal. Walk around: the ring fills and the phone pulses faster as you get closer. It cannot find a camera that joined someone else's Wi-Fi.

The report

  • A fingerprint

    The PDF report carries a code worked out from everything in it, photos included. If anyone changes the report later, the code will no longer match.

Limits

  • No app finds every camera. A lens behind dark plastic or at a sharp angle may not reflect enough. Cameras without night-vision lights, on mobile data, or recording to a memory card can stay hidden from these checks. A result worth a closer look is a reason to look closely, not proof.
