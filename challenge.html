Wi-Fi test

Flash a secret pattern at the object. If it's a camera that streams, its Wi-Fi traffic will follow the pattern.

Step 1

Flash the pattern

Start the Mac Sniffer first, then point this screen at the object for about 25 seconds. The screen will flash.

How to record Wi-Fi on the Mac
  1. Open Wireless Diagnostics → Window → Sniffer, pick the channel, press Start.
  2. Run the pattern from 0.5–2 m away.
  3. Press Stop. The file is saved in /var/tmp/.
Step 2

Drop the capture

Drop a .pcap or .pcapng file, or click to choose
