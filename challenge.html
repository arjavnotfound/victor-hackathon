Wi-Fi challenge

The screen flashes a secret pattern at the suspicious object. A streaming camera sends more data when its picture changes, so its Wi-Fi traffic follows the pattern. Nothing else in the room can.

1. Run the challenge

  1. On the Mac: open Wireless Diagnostics → Window → Sniffer, pick the channel and press Start.
  2. Point this screen at the object from 0.5 to 2 m, as close to its "eye" as you can.
  3. Press Start. Keep the screen pointed at it for about 25 seconds.
  4. Stop the Sniffer. The file is saved in /var/tmp/.

The screen will flash. Do not look at it if flashing lights affect you.

12 bits × 2 s, plus a 3 s countdown
