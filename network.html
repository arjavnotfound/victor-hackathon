Network

Looks at Wi-Fi networks around you, and at devices on the Wi-Fi you joined, for ones that behave like cameras.

  • Needs the Android app

    Web pages are not allowed to look at Wi-Fi networks or other devices. The lens and night-vision checks work here.

  • 0nearby networks
  • 0devices on this Wi-Fi

    • Check this Wi-Fi and the air around you

      Join the room's Wi-Fi first if you can. Android will ask for location, which it needs to list nearby networks.

    Some Wi-Fi networks keep devices hidden from each other, and cameras can use mobile data or record to a memory card. An empty list does not prove a room is clear.
